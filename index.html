Heritage / Filed 1974

Rugby is a sport of inches and decades. We make clothing that respects both — built once, worn for a generation.

The H Connection began in a garage in San Isidro, Buenos Aires, with a single embroidery machine and a stubborn belief that the rituals of rugby — the singing, the standing, the shaking of hands — deserve a wardrobe of their own. We do not chase seasonal cycles. We file new drops only when the craft demands it.

Every garment passes through three hands before it ships: the cutter, the embroiderer, and the inspector. Country collections are built in close conversation with players, supporters, and the unions themselves.

Filed by Hugo Etcheverry · Founder — SAN ISIDRO · 04.62

N° 01

Organic Cotton
— certified at the bale.

Every garment begins with GOTS-certified organic cotton, sourced from a single co-operative in northern Argentina. No synthetic blends. No micro-plastics. The hand-feel is dense, the weight is honest.

GSM RANGE 180 — 320
N° 02

Embroidered Crests
— never printed.

Crests are stitched on Tajima machines with up to nine thread colours. Each badge takes between 14 and 22 minutes to complete. The reverse is bound with a satin under-stitch so the inside is as finished as the outside.

STITCH COUNT 8.4K — 22.1K
N° 03

Lifetime Repair
— ship it back, free.

A torn collar, a frayed hem, a missing button. Send the garment to our atelier. We mend it, return it, no charge — for as long as we exist. The serial number sewn into the inner seam is your guarantee.

Leinster hold off Toulon (29-25) and return to the Champions Cup final

At the Aviva Stadium in Dublin, Leinster withstood a French comeback to qualify for the 23 May final in Bilbao. Tries from Conan, van der Flier, Ringrose and Doris; Harry Byrne added 3 conversions and a penalty. They await the winner of Bath vs Bordeaux-Bègles, who meet on Sunday.

New tournament with 12 nations in two pools (Americas/Pacific and Europe/Asia/Africa). Two windows in July and November, round-robin format between pools. First match: Uruguay vs Georgia at Estadio Charrúa.
