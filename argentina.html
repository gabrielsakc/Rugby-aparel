05 / The Crest

The Pumas, in 12,400 stitches.

Every Argentina garment carries an embroidered Pumas crest at the heart — never printed, never sublimated. The crest is rendered in six thread colours on a Tajima TMEZ machine, with a satin under-stitch on the reverse so the inside finishes as cleanly as the outside.

Each crest takes between 14 and 18 minutes to complete. The serial number sewn into the inner seam guarantees lifetime repair — torn collars, frayed hems, missing buttons. Send the garment to the atelier; it returns mended, no charge.

Heritage / Filed 2007

We are not the favourites. We never have been. That is the point.

Los Pumas have spent a century playing with their backs to the wall — outscored, outfunded, outnumbered. They have answered every time with a song, a hand on the chest, and a refusal to leave the field early. This catalogue is for that posture.

The 2007 Rugby World Cup, the 2022 win in New Zealand, the 2023 semi-final in Marseille — they were not about results. They were about showing up dressed for it. We make the wardrobe of that ritual.
